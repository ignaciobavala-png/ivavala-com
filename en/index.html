❓ 04

FAQ

How long does it take?

A one-page institutional site, one to two weeks. A store or a custom system depends on scope. The deadline is set before we start and does not move unless the scope changes.

What does “maintenance included” mean?

That I do not disappear after delivery. For the first 3 months it covers keeping the site online, security updates and fixing anything that breaks because of something I did, at no cost. If something goes down, I reply within 24 hours.

If we stop working together, am I left with nothing?

No. The domain, the code and the access are yours from day one. If you want to continue with another developer, they take everything and do not start from scratch. I do not work with closed platforms you cannot leave.

Do I need the text and photos ready before starting?

No. We can begin with whatever you have and fill in the rest along the way; if you need it, I help you work out what to say. The only thing I need from the start is to understand what your business does and who you sell to.

Do you work with clients in other countries?

Yes. I am based in Argentina and work remotely across any time zone. I speak Spanish and English, and email is the main channel.

How do we start?

Tell me what you need in three lines and I send you a proposal with a fixed price and deadline. No strings: if it does not work for you, nothing happens.

Still have a question that is not here? Tell me about your project and I reply today.
