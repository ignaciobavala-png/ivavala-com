Privacy policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026.

Who is responsible

Ignacio Vavala, responsible for ivavala.com. Contact: ignacio@ivavala.com.

What data we collect

  • Contact form, booking and chat assistant: your name, your email or WhatsApp, the plan you are interested in and whatever you write in notes or in the chat.
  • Where your visit came from: if you arrive from an ad or a link with parameters (such as gclid or utm_*), we store them in your browser to know which channel worked.
  • Site usage: aggregate visit and event metrics (for example, opening the booking form or clicking WhatsApp).

What we use it for

To answer your enquiries, schedule the video call and send you the calendar invitation with the Meet link, and to measure and improve the site and advertising. We do not sell your data or use it for anything else.

Who it is shared with

  • Cloudflare: hosting, database and anti-spam verification (Turnstile).
  • Resend and Gmail: delivery of notification and invitation emails.
  • Groq: processes the chat assistant messages to generate replies. Do not type sensitive data in the chat.
  • Google (Ads): advertising conversion measurement.

Cookies and local storage

The site uses browser storage to remember where your visit came from and Google cookies (Google Ads and Google Analytics) to measure ad conversions and site usage. You can block them in your browser settings without losing access to the content.

How long we keep it

Enquiry and booking data is kept as long as needed to assist you and for a maximum of 24 months, unless you ask for deletion earlier or a business relationship begins.

Your rights

You can request access, correction or deletion of your data by writing to ignacio@ivavala.com. Under Argentina's Personal Data Protection Law 25,326, the data subject may exercise the right of access free of charge at intervals of no less than six months. The Agency for Access to Public Information (AAIP) is the supervisory authority and handles complaints about non-compliance.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page is updated with the new date.
